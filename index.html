About Me

I am Ruotian Peng (彭若天), a Ph.D. student at Zhejiang University and Westlake University, advised by Prof. Yandong Wen. I received my B.E. degree in Computer Science from South China University of Technology. I previously interned at Huawei Foundation Model Lab, and I am currently interning at Alibaba's Token Foundry.

My research focuses on large language models and AI agents, with a broad interest in improving their reasoning and self-improvement capabilities. I am particularly interested in recursive self-improvement (VibeTraining-Bench), enabling models to explore more effectively through reinforcement learning (SimKO, MOSA), improving formal reasoning so that model outputs become more reliable and verifiable (FormalMATH), and studying continual learning in real-world settings where models must adapt without forgetting (PEFT-Arena). Previously, I also worked on multimodal generation (Patch Matters, OpenStory++).

I am currently interested in recursive self-improvement (RSI), agent RL, and tool-use RL. I believe that language models can develop stronger capabilities by interacting with external environments, tools, and feedback—and by using that experience to iteratively improve their own training and inference systems.

Selected Papers

1. VibeTraining-Bench: Data–Trainer–Harness Co-Design for Iterative System Improvement
Ruotian Peng*, Yuanyi Ren*, Shuaiqi Liu, Xin Guo, Fang Zhu, Yangyi Huang, Yue Wu, Zheng Wang, Yandong Wen†, Zhihang Fu†

arXiv 2026

We introduce a benchmark for recursive self-improvement that evaluates whether research agents can diagnose system bottlenecks and iteratively co-design training data, trainers, and inference harnesses across terminal operation, software engineering, multi-turn tool use, and mathematical reasoning tasks.

2. Preserving Exploration for LLM Reasoning via Mean Order-Statistic Alignment
Ruotian Peng*, Yi Ren*, Longfei Li, Yandong Wen†

NeurIPS 2026

We argue that token-wise KL is too local to preserve sequence-level exploration, and propose MOSA to align the model's global exploration behavior through a mean order-statistic profile, improving both pass@1 and pass@K across reasoning and coding benchmarks.

3. SimKO: Simple Pass@K Policy Optimization
Ruotian Peng*, Yi Ren*, Zhouliang Yu, Weiyang Liu, Yandong Wen†

Findings of EMNLP 2026

We introduce a new perspective for understanding RLVR dynamics by using top-N probabilities as a tractable approximation of the full token-level distribution, and propose a simple objective that preserves output diversity and improves exploration for LLM reasoning.

4. Patch Matters: Training-free Fine-grained Image Caption Enhancement via Local Perception
Ruotian Peng*, Haiying He*, Yake Wei, Yandong Wen, Di Hu†

CVPR 2025

We enhance image captioning without training by decomposing images into local patches, extracting fine-grained details, and aggregating them into more faithful long-form descriptions.

5. FormalMATH: Benchmarking Formal Mathematical Reasoning of Large Language Models
Zhouliang Yu*, Ruotian Peng*, Keyi Ding*, Yizhe Li, Zhongyuan Peng, Minghao Liu, Yifan Zhang, Zheng Yuan, Huajian Xin, Wenhao Huang, Yandong Wen, Ge Zhang, Weiyang Liu†

Findings of EMNLP 2026

We build a large-scale Lean4 benchmark for formal mathematical reasoning and evaluate current LLM-based theorem provers across diverse mathematical domains.
